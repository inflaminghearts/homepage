Description

Inflaming Hearts is an indie game studio from Brazil. We create original games, run cultural events for game developers, especially game jams, and team up with other small studios through co-development (co-dev), a more sustainable way to make games.

History

Inflaming Hearts was founded in December 2022 and is Narto Junior's vision for game development, shaped by more than a decade in the independent game community of Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil.

The vision behind it

Narto Junior holds a technologist degree in Systems Analysis and Development and a specialization in Digital Games, and is currently studying Cultural Production. Since 2015, he has co-founded and organized PONG – Potiguar Indie Games, a game developer community in Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil, and he serves as a Global Game Jam regional organizer. Programmer, producer and game designer.
