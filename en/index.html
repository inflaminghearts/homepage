About

Inflaming Hearts was born to make game development sustainable. We believe in co-development (co-dev): small studios joining forces to bring projects to life, without losing the creative process that only an independent studio can offer.

The vision behind it

Inflaming Hearts is Narto Junior's vision for game development. Narto holds a technologist degree in Systems Analysis and Development and a specialization in Digital Games, and is currently studying Cultural Production.

Since 2015, he has co-founded and organized PONG – Potiguar Indie Games, a game developer community in Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil, and he serves as a Global Game Jam regional organizer. Programmer, producer and game designer.
